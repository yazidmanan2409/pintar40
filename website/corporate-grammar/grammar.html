Same family, different personality

The PINTAR application is calm, quiet, predictable and focused. That is right for a tool people work in every day. The corporate website has a different job: attract → explain → demonstrate → build confidence → invite action. It needs more energy and character, while staying sophisticated, trustworthy and restrained.

Kept from the familyDifferent, on purpose
The supplied PINTAR SELALU mark, unaltered, and its own teal (#224F55) as the brand anchorDisplay typography at 4–6.5 rem, where the app's largest heading is about 1.75 rem
Plus Jakarta Sans for reading text, from the product's own font filesWeights beyond the app's 400/600; in Direction A, a serif for display
The product's own words for every report, and its evidence semantics: neutral bars, and a figure shown only when the evidence is enoughDark bands, an accent colour and photography: things the app deliberately avoids
Bahasa Indonesia first; nothing stored on the visitor's deviceEntrance motion and asymmetric, editorial composition

Not taken from the app: its tokens, components, layout shell or navigation. This grammar has its own (assets/grammar.css).

01

Visual personality

Three directions, each a different answer to "where does the energy come from?". All three obey every invariant rule in this document. Open each full composition to judge them.

A

Editorial

Bukti, dibaca dengan tenang.

An intelligent education journal. Energy comes from typography and scale: a serif display, hairline rules, numbered figures with captions, documentary photography. Reports appear as captioned figures on paper.
B

Instrument

Bukti yang terang.

Precise and data-confident. Energy comes from contrast and evidence: deep night-teal canvases, heavy sans display, mono labels, report artefacts staggered as the hero image, and true facts set as large numbers.
C

Human

Untuk setiap orang di sekolah.

Warm and people-first. Energy comes from colour and photography: sand and teal colour blocks, a terracotta accent, rounded containers, a person beside their evidence. It depends most on real photography.

The same for every direction: never

Loud, flashy, childish, startup-gimmicky, animation-heavy, driven by gradients or effects, full of decorative UI, or a clone of the PINTAR application.

02

Colour strategy

Colours are given by role, not by name. Brand colour and evidence colour are different tokens and never stand in for each other.

  1. The brand anchor is the mark's own teal, #224F55, sampled from the supplied logo. The app uses #245F55. They are close relatives, deliberately not identical (see Decisions).
  2. The accent covers at most about 10% of any screen. It marks; it does not fill. Exception: Direction C's primary call to action.
  3. Evidence is never painted in brand or accent colours. Every bar uses one neutral ink, as the product draws it. A band such as "Perlu penguatan" is never red, and "Mantap" is never green.
  4. Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 and large text at least 3:1, calculated rather than judged by eye. A's ochre and C's terracotta fall below 4.5:1 on their sunken grounds, so they are used only for text of 24 px or larger, for fills and for marks. Where A needs ochre in small text (a caption number, a note mark) it uses a deeper step, #7F570F, which clears 5:1.
  5. There are at most two surface families on a page: light (paper and paper-sunk) and one dark band colour.
  6. No gradients, glows, glassmorphism or colour washes over photographs. Text over imagery always sits on a solid ground.

04

Spacing and rhythm

One 4-px-based scale. Sections breathe at 64–128 px, so a scroll reads as a sequence of single ideas.

4 · 8 · 12 · 16
24 · 32 · 48
64 · 96 · 128
  1. Section padding is fluid from 64 to 128 px (--section-y). A section's heading has 64 px below it before its content.
  2. Inside a block, spacing steps down: 24 px between paragraphs and controls, 12–16 px inside cards.
  3. One idea per screen on desktop. If two ideas share a viewport, one of them is a figure.
05

Page width and grid

A 12-column grid in a 76 rem (1216 px) content width, with a fluid gutter of 16–48 px. On a phone it is four columns.

  1. Reading measure is 38 rem (about 65 characters of Indonesian). Text never spans the full width.
  2. Asymmetric splits: 7/5 and 5/7, never 6/6, for a hero or any text-plus-figure pair. The 6/6 split is reserved for comparisons.
  3. Dark and sunken bands bleed to the viewport edge; their content stays in the grid.
  4. Figures may break the grid's rhythm (a vertical offset, or a report laid over a photograph) only in heroes and report sections. A figure never covers text, a call to action, or another figure's data.

07

Hero grammar

Say what PINTAR is and why it matters in one screen, and offer the experience. The three directions compose it differently; the contents are the same.

  1. Five parts, no more: label, headline (≤ 10 words), lead (≤ 30 words), one primary call to action plus one secondary link, and one visual.
  2. The visual is evidence, not decoration: a real report artefact, or (in C) a documentary photograph with an artefact beside it. Never an abstract illustration, a device mockup, or a stock photo.
  3. The primary call to action is always "Coba Mini-PINTAR", and the secondary is "Lihat laporannya".
  4. No carousel, no rotating headline, no video background.

09

Imagery and product screenshots

Two kinds of picture: photographs of real school life, and PINTAR's own screens. Neither is ever invented.

PHOTOGRAPHDocumentary, in real Indonesian classrooms, in natural light. Never posed and never stock. 3:2 or 4:5. Labelled until real photography exists.
  1. Photography is documentary: real schools, real light, people doing the work. No AI-generated images, no robots, brains, holograms or glowing networks, and no laptop stock photography.
  2. Product screens are drawn as live artefacts (HTML, as here) rather than bitmap screenshots wherever possible. They stay sharp, translatable and accessible, and they cannot drift from the product's words unnoticed.
  3. When a bitmap screenshot is unavoidable, it is the real product with simulated data, labelled, and shown full and flat. No fake laptops or phones, no perspective tilt, and never cropped through text.
  4. Direction C depends on photography. Until a shoot exists it should not be judged on its placeholders alone.
10

Reports and evidence: the hero capability

What PINTAR can tell people after learning happens is its strongest story, so the reports themselves are the website's main visual material. Every artefact below is a real PINTAR screen in the product's own words. Only the numbers are simulated.

  1. The artefact is the product's; the frame is the site's. Titles, labels, meanings, band names and state sentences are copied from the product. The caption ("Gambar 2 — …") and annotations ("i · Untuk guru") are the site's voice, and sit outside the artefact.
  2. Every artefact says DATA SIMULASI, and says who sees it ("Dilihat oleh guru kelas ini").
  3. Show the refusals too. "Belum cukup data untuk menampilkan angka ini." is part of the message, not an embarrassment.
  4. Neutral bars, always. Bands are never coloured as verdicts. Every chart has a table twin for screen readers.
  5. One artefact per idea, with at most five on the landing page. The rest live on the Laporan page.
  6. Only screens that exist. Not featured, because they are not live or not ruled in scope: periodic briefings, automatic Question Health signals, the recurring-misconception list (empty in practice), student feedback on teachers, and any parent view. There is no AI anywhere in PINTAR's reports, and the site must never say there is.

Audience benefits

Each audience answers "what becomes better for me?" in at most three outcomes, each a bold lead phrase plus at most 12 words. No essays and no feature lists.

Sekolah

  • Satu gambaran pembelajaran, tanpa peringkat orang.
  • Tahu angka mana yang sudah boleh ditafsirkan.

Guru

  • Soal yang sulit, dan siapa yang mungkin perlu bantuan.
  • Satu langkah yang paling layak dikerjakan lebih dulu.

Murid

  • Mulai dari apa yang sudah diketahui.
  • Hasil yang jujur, dengan yang perlu dipelajari lagi.

Orang tua

  • Sertifikat yang bisa diperiksa siapa pun.

Decision needed: see below.

Decision: Parents

PINTAR has no parent role, screen, report or notification. "Parent experience" is on the product's LATER list, and the parent role-view ruling R1 says nothing was built. What is true today is indirect: public certificate verification (/verifikasi, which does not show the holder's name), and results that are never overstated. Options: (1) keep Parents, limited to those indirect benefits, as drafted; (2) leave Parents off until a parent experience exists; (3) show Parents with an "under consideration" status. I recommend (1) or (2), not (3): the website must not present roadmap as product.

17

Iconography

  1. One set, drawn on a 24 px grid: a 1.6 stroke, round caps, no fills, in currentColor.
  2. An icon labels an idea next to its words, and never replaces them. No emoji, no illustration in place of an icon, no icon in body text.
  3. The chip around an icon follows the direction: A a hairline circle, B a soft square, C a pale rounded square.

Motion

Motion supports hierarchy and discovery. It happens once, on entry, and then everything is still.

TokenValueUsed for
--easecubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.32, 1)everything: a quick start that settles gently

Your device asks for reduced motion, so nothing above moves: everything is simply there. That is the reduced-motion behaviour.

  1. Entrance only, once. An element fades and rises the first time it enters the viewport, and never moves again. Report bars grow from zero once.
  2. Never: blinking, looping, autoplaying carousels, bouncing, scroll-jacking, typing effects, counters that spin up, or decoration that moves on its own.
  3. Parallax: not used. Depth comes from offsets and shadow (Direction B's stack), not from motion tied to scrolling. If ever wanted, it is one layer, at most 24 px of travel, and off for reduced motion.
  4. Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion): no translation and no fade. Everything is present at load, and state changes are instant.
  5. Without JavaScript, everything is visible. Content is hidden for its entrance only when the script is running and motion is allowed.
19

Responsive behaviour

  1. Breakpoints at 640, 760, 900, 960, 1024 and 1100 px, chosen where the content breaks rather than by device. Nothing overflows sideways from 320 to 1440 px.
  2. Splits stack text first, figure second. In A the report wall becomes one column; in B the stack keeps one or two artefacts; C's role pills stay.
  3. Four audience cards become two, then one. Capability details stay collapsed.
  4. No horizontal carousels on phones. Fewer artefacts, each shown whole, with a link to the Laporan page.

20

Accessibility

  1. Contrast is measured, not judged: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and for marks that carry meaning, at both widths. verify/check.mjs measures every page in all three directions.
  2. Nothing is smaller than 14 px. Targets are at least 44 px.
  3. Focus is always visible: a 3 px ring, with a light ring on dark bands. There is a skip link on every page.
  4. Every chart has a table twin. Each artefact is a named group, and the role pills are real buttons with aria-pressed.
  5. Headings are in order, one H1 per page, and lang is set (id or en).
  6. Reduced motion and no-JavaScript both give a complete, static page. On the site the report artefacts are plain HTML. In this review package one shared script draws them, so that every page shows the same source; without JavaScript the package shows the text but not the artefacts.
  7. No text is set in an image. Text over a photograph sits on a solid ground.

What not to do

✕ Bands as verdicts

Red and green turn a band into a judgement about children. The product refuses this, and so does the site.

✓ One neutral ink
Mantap9
Perlu penguatan7

The distribution reads as a shape, not an alarm.

✕ Gradient-and-glow hero

Revolusi belajar dengan AI ✨

Effects instead of evidence, and a claim (AI) PINTAR does not make.

The product's own screen does the persuading.

✕ Invented numbers

23

Decisions for you

These are not mine to settle. Nothing propagates until you rule.

  1. Direction: A, B or C, or a named combination (for example, A's typography with B's report stack). My recommendation is in the overview.
  2. Brand teal: the mark's own #224F55 for the website, or the app's approved #245F55.
  3. A display serif (Direction A only): Newsreader (OFL) would revise the 24 Sep identity ruling that the site uses Plus Jakarta Sans.
  4. Parents: options (1), (2) or (3) in section 11.
  5. PT ZPS: the full legal name for © and Tentang kami, and the contact details.
  6. Photography: whether a documentary shoot is planned. It decides how far Direction C can go.
