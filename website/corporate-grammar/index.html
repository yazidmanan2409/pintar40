A design grammar for the website, before the website.

A fresh visual language for the corporate site, built from PINTAR's real capability and its own report screens. It is a different personality from the application, from the same family. Nothing here has been applied to the existing site, to Mini-PINTAR or to the application.

03

My recommendation

RECOMMENDATION, NOT A RULING

A · Editorial as the base, borrowing one thing from B: the staggered report stack for the reports section.

  • It is the furthest from the application while staying calm. A serif display and captioned figures say "read this"; the app says "work here". "Same family, different personality" is visible at first glance.
  • Reports become the picture without looking like a dashboard. A captioned figure on paper is evidence presented, not software demonstrated.
  • It is complete without photography. No shoot exists yet; C cannot be judged fairly until one does.
  • Restraint is built in: one accent used for marks only, hairlines instead of boxes, one dark band per page.
  • From B, the stack: three real screens side by side at different heights, each labelled with who sees it, is the strongest single image of "one evidence, three readers". It sits inside A's reports band without changing A's voice.

The cost: A adds Newsreader (OFL; about 120 KB for the upright and italic Latin files) and revises the 24 September ruling that the site uses Plus Jakarta Sans only. If you would rather keep that ruling, B is my second choice, because its energy comes from contrast and the reports rather than from a new typeface.

04

Decisions for you

These are not mine to settle. Each changes what gets built next.

  1. Direction: A, B or C, or a named combination (my recommendation is above).
  2. Brand teal: the mark's own #224F55 for the website, or the application's approved #245F55. They are close relatives, deliberately not identical.
  3. A display serif: only if A is chosen. Newsreader would revise the 24 September identity ruling.
  4. Parents: PINTAR has no parent role, screen, report or notification; the parent experience is on the LATER list. Options: (1) keep Parents, limited to what is true today (certificates anyone can verify, results that are never overstated); (2) leave Parents off until a parent experience exists; (3) "under consideration". I recommend (1) or (2), never (3).
  5. PT ZPS: the full legal name for the © line and Tentang kami, the address and the contact details. All are marked [konfirmasi] for now.
  6. Photography: whether a documentary shoot in real classrooms is planned, and when. It decides how far Direction C, or photographs in A, can go.
05

The capability boundary

Every report shown is a real PINTAR screen in the product's own words, checked against the product's text source. Only the numbers are simulated, and every artefact says so.

Featured, because it is live

  • Apa yang sudah bisa disimpulkan: the teacher's class evidence (/mengajar/[kelas]/bukti)
  • Soal mana yang sulit bagi kelas ini: per-question view with who may need help (/mengajar/[kelas]/soal)
  • Penguasaan per bagian, and Sebaran hasil Uji Penguasaan with the four fixed bands
  • "Belum cukup data": the refusal to show a figure below its minimum
  • Yang didukung bukti: the head of department's and dean's view (/pimpinan)
  • Hasil: the student's own result (/belajar/[tugas]/hasil)
  • Verifikasi Sertifikat: public, without the holder's name (/verifikasi/[token])

Deliberately not featured

  • Question Health: the metrics have no screen
  • Misconceptions: the list is likely empty in practice
  • Briefings: no screen
  • Anything for parents: not built (see decision 4)
  • Student feedback on teachers: built, but not in scope and not in production
  • AI: there is none in PINTAR, so the site never implies it
  • Adoption figures: PINTAR is not production-ready and no school uses it in production yet, so there are no user, school or outcome numbers

Noticed on the way

The paused corporate site in website/corporate is now behind the product. Its own drift check (verify/canonical.mjs) reports nine "reconciliation required" problems against the current main branch: the readiness bands were renamed (Mantap, Berkembang, Perlu penguatan, Belum mengerjakan Uji Penguasaan; «Sebaran hasil Uji Penguasaan»), and three new migrations write to the catalogue. The check is doing its job. Nothing was changed there; whichever direction is chosen, the new site will be composed from the product as it is now.

07

What was not touched

Mini-PINTAR, the PINTAR application, the paused corporate site and the application's design system are unchanged. Nothing has been deployed. This folder is a review package: its links to Mini-PINTAR point at the existing one, and its copy is draft, written only to make the compositions readable.
