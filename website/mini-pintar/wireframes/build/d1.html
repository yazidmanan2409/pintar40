1 · Pemetaan Awal 3 questions, no score, skippable
Pemetaan Awal

Ini bukan ujian. Jawaban Anda dipakai untuk menyusun urutan bacaan yang sesuai.

Sebuah laporan sekolah menulis: “85% siswa lulus ujian akhir.”

Apa yang paling perlu Anda ketahui berikutnya?

A   Berapa siswa yang mengikuti ujian itu.
B   Berapa persentasenya tahun lalu.
C   Siapa yang mengajar kelas itu.
D   Berapa nilai rata-ratanya.
Berikutnya

Lewati

Interaction & behaviour

  1. Real radio group — arrow keys move within it. Choosing does not auto-advance; the visitor confirms.
  2. Dot progress, never a percentage. Text equivalent present for AT.
  3. No right/wrong feedback at any point in this stage. The absence is itself part of the demonstration.
  4. Lewati is a first-class action. Skipping is evidence — an honest “I don't know” — and §4.1 treats it as such.
  5. Back is permitted and the previous answer is shown and changeable. Nothing is submitted, so nothing is being gamed.

A11y: step announced by aria-live="polite" (“Pemetaan Awal, pertanyaan 2 dari 3”). 56px targets.

Buka pelajarannya

Interaction & behaviour

  1. The payoff moment. First place the visitor sees their own answers change something.
  2. Each card names their thinking, not their score — one of nine misconception-specific reasons (§3.3).
  3. Order is derived, deterministic, and severity-ranked D2 > D3 > D1. No randomness anywhere in the journey.
  4. A right answer is named too, as an observed answer and never as mastery — the domain gets a SUDAH_TEPAT card rather than disappearing.
  5. All-correct path is designed: no weakness is manufactured; the reading offered deepens rather than repairs.

A11y: vertical order is the meaning, so it survives narrowing untouched. Reorder animation removed under reduced motion.

Kapan sebuah kesimpulan boleh diambil

Setiap kesimpulan berdiri di atas dua hal: berapa banyak yang diamati, dan berapa lama. Tiga siswa selama satu minggu memberi kita sesuatu, tetapi belum cukup untuk memisahkan…

Contoh bertahap
Berapa yang cukup?
1   Berapa banyak yang diamati? Tiga.
Tampilkan langkah berikutnya
Lanjut ke Uji Penguasaan

Interaction & behaviour

  1. Three lessons exist; the visitor reads one. That is what makes the reordering meaningful rather than decorative.
  2. Reading, not video. ~120 words, one worked example.
  3. Staged reveal — the product's own interaction, one step at a time.
  4. The button stays above the fold after each reveal on mobile.

A11y: revealed steps appended to a aria-live="polite" list; static full reveal under reduced motion.
