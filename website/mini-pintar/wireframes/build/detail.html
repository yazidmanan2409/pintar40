Why this is the outcome that matters

  1. Reachable by the commonest route. One of two → BELUM_CUKUP. The honest refusal is the ordinary result, not an edge case kept for the brochure.
  2. The evidence limit appears in all three outcomes, including the best one — especially the best one.
  3. No score, no percentage, no pass/fail. Belum, never tidak.
  4. No delta, and it says why. Refusing to compute a flattering number is a stronger demonstration than the number would have been.

A11y: outcome is an h1; the limit statement is ordinary prose, not a visually-hidden footnote.
