4 · Uji Penguasaan 2 questions, disjoint from stage 1
Uji Penguasaan
ID | EN   Keluar
Waktu berjalan 00:42Tandai

Sebuah program baru dicoba di dua kelas selama satu semester, dan hasilnya membaik.

Mana yang paling menambah kekuatan buktinya?

A   Membandingkan dengan kelas serupa yang tidak mengikuti program.
B   Menambah jumlah kelas yang mengikuti.
🧭 Perlu arah?  — tersedia sejak awal, dan muncul sendiri setelah jeda
Berikutnya

Interaction & behaviour

  1. Time counts up, never down, and is decorative: aria-hidden, no limit imposed.
  2. Help is not gated behind a timer. The pill appears after ~20s of inactivity and a persistent Perlu arah? button is there from the start — so an AT user never races a clock.
  3. Two staged levels: ARAH, then PETUNJUK. Neither names nor narrows to the correct option.
  4. Zero item overlap with Pemetaan Awal, so no “improvement” can be an artefact of having seen the question.
  5. Inline pill, never a modal.

A11y: pill announced politely and does not take focus. Removing the timing dependency also removes WCAG 2.2.1 entirely.

5 · Hasil the BELUM_CUKUP outcome shown
HasilID | EN   Keluar

Belum cukup bukti.

Dua pertanyaan belum cukup untuk menyimpulkan penguasaan. Itu bukan kekurangan Anda — itu batas buktinya.

Batas bukti
Ini cukup untuk satu bacaan kecil. Belum cukup untuk menilai penguasaan seseorang.
Pemetaan Awal dan Uji Penguasaan menanyakan hal yang berbeda, jadi keduanya tidak dibandingkan.
Lihat apa yang barusan terjadi

Interaction & behaviour

  1. Reachable by the commonest route. One of two → BELUM_CUKUP. The honest refusal is the ordinary result, not an edge case kept for the brochure.
  2. The evidence limit appears in all three outcomes, including the best one — especially the best one.
  3. No score, no percentage, no pass/fail. Belum, never tidak.
  4. No delta, and it says why. Refusing to compute a flattering number is a stronger demonstration than the number would have been.

A11y: outcome is an h1; the limit statement is ordinary prose, not a visually-hidden footnote.

Interaction & behaviour

  1. The path they walked, annotated with the mechanism rather than the feature name.
  2. Vertical on both desktop and mobile. A horizontal diagram that becomes vertical is two diagrams; the vertical one is better on both.
  3. R9 held in the grammar: PINTAR menunjukkan and membantu; the human memilih. The system never holds the deciding verb.

A11y: an ordered list, not a decorative graphic. Readable in full without CSS.

Yang barusan adalah miniatur publik: bukan asesmen resmi, tidak menghasilkan sertifikat, dan tidak meninggalkan catatan tentang Anda.

honesty statement 2 of 2

Interaction & behaviour

  1. Three doors, because a single CTA wastes the one moment the visitor is most willing to go further by guessing which way they want to go.
  2. Second honesty statement, where it reads as integrity rather than as a disclaimer wall.
  3. Keluar mid-journey returns to the homepage at the invitation section, not the top — the visitor resumes where they left the argument.

A11y: doors are links, not buttons, and are reachable in reading order before the honesty note.
