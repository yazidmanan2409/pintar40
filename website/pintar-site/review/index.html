PINTAR public website · Task 01 · first visual review

The website, before it is published.

Every page is designed and built, in Bahasa Indonesia and English. This page explains the decisions in brief, maps every visual to its section, and lists what needs your ruling. Nothing has been deployed.

1. Creative direction

Typography leads. Newsreader, a contemporary serif, carries what is said: large, light and set with deliberate line breaks. Inter, the typeface of PINTAR’s own wordmark and reports, carries what is read and operated. The wordmark is the text-only «PINTAR» in Inter Bold, #075056, 0.14 em tracking (D-192, D-193).

Colour is quiet. Warm white #F6F4EF, a soft neutral #EDE9E1, charcoal #1B1E1D, and the brand teal #075056 used sparingly, with one deep-teal band per page at most. No gradients, decorative icons or stock illustration.

Images explain. Documentary photographs of Indonesian schools show people. Native diagrams show how PINTAR works, and each replaces paragraphs. There are no product screenshots yet and none are faked: the one evidence picture is labelled as a concept illustration.

Layout. A 12-column grid with a maximum width of 86 rem and wide margins. Splits are asymmetric (5/7, 7/5), with hairline rules instead of cards, and one focal point per section.

Motion. Text and figures rise 18 px and fade in once (680 ms). Diagram stages light up once, in sequence. There is no parallax, autoplay, looping or scroll hijacking. With reduced motion everything is present and still; without JavaScript every page is complete.

Inspiration, not imitation. From Leica: precision, confident photography and captioned editorial composition (the viewfinder marks on photo frames are a quiet nod). From MUJI: warmth, honesty, whitespace and restraint. No marks, layouts or assets are copied.

6. Mobile and bilingual behaviour

  • Responsive: every page is checked for horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px. Diagrams reflow from horizontal to vertical; photographs switch to their phone crop (mostly 4:5).
  • Phone navigation: on a phone the navigation is a full-screen menu with large serif links, and the language switch stays visible.
  • Bilingual copy: both languages are written, not translated; Indonesian runs longer, and the layouts are tested in it. Terms follow the universal vocabulary (Peserta didik, Pendidik, Pimpinan, Institusi), pending the written terminology decision.
  • Accessibility:
    • measured contrast, no text under 14 px, controls of 44 px or more, visible focus, and a skip link;
    • one H1 per page, alternative text on every photograph, diagrams as real lists, form errors linked to their fields with a summary, and status in words, not colour alone.

7. Implementation, and what needs your approval

Build. The site is static, with a zero-dependency Node build (node build.mjs) that writes plain HTML to site/; the result uploads to the existing Namecheap hosting as it is. Pages are under 20 KB of HTML each, with one stylesheet (47 KB), one small script (8 KB) and self-hosted fonts (Inter and Newsreader, about 170 KB on a first visit, then cached).

Contact form. api/kontak.php (PHP 8.1 or later, already available on that hosting) does all of the following on the server:

  • validates every field and refuses foreign origins;
  • stops bots with a honeypot and a timing check;
  • limits each sender to five messages an hour and ignores double submits;
  • moderates content in Indonesian and English;
  • delivers by email to info@pintarselalu.com.

Credentials, if SMTP is used, live in a config file outside the public folder.

Moderation. Moderation is rule-based and configurable:

  • normalisation defeats disguises such as look-alike letters, zero-width characters, l33t spelling, spaced or stretched letters and full-width text;
  • clear abuse is refused with your exact wording;
  • reports, quotations and borderline messages are delivered marked «PERLU DITINJAU», never discarded;
  • nothing of a refused message is kept, only the time, a category and a hashed address, for 30 days.

The 47 acceptance cases cover both languages and all three outcomes.

Decisions for you

1 · Public status of 4.0. The site labels 4.0 “Fondasi · verifikasi rilis / Foundation · in release verification”, because no release has been accepted for production. Once 4.0 is accepted, one constant changes it to “available”. Please confirm the wording, and who confirms the switch.
2 · Photography. Approve a commissioned documentary shoot (eight images, two regions), and the consent process for minors (parental consent forms) and staff.
3 · Company facts for the legal pages.
  • Registered address and NIB.
  • Hosting and email providers, and whether any data leaves Indonesia.
  • How long the mailbox keeps email.
  • Whether a data protection officer is appointed.
  • Trademark status.
  • An effective date.
Whether the website needs a PSE (private electronic system operator) registration should be checked by counsel. Both legal pages are drafts until a qualified reviewer approves them.
4 · Mail delivery. Either use the host’s mail() with SPF and DKIM set for pintarselalu.com (no password), or an authenticated SMTP mailbox (more reliable). Either way, a test message must be confirmed in the real inbox before launch.
5 · Domain and indexing. Every page is noindex while in review. Publication (node build.mjs --publish) needs the production domain, for canonical and hreflang URLs, and your go-ahead. I have not deployed anything.
6 · Mini-PINTAR and the paused corporate site. Per the brief, the new site does not link to Mini-PINTAR. Should Mini-PINTAR stay online elsewhere, or be retired with the paused corporate site once this one is published?
7 · Repository history (not a design matter). Main was rewritten (a new 368-commit history from 9 October). This website lane still carries the old history, which may include what the rewrite removed. With your approval I can move the website work onto the new main and replace the lane.
