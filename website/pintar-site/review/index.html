PINTAR public website · review build 2 · launch wording for PINTAR 4.1

The revised website, still unpublished.

This build carries the intended launch wording for when PINTAR 4.1 has been accepted by Dr Gladys and her team. Preparing the wording does not mean acceptance has occurred. Every page is noindex and carries a review strip, and nothing has been deployed. Production deployment still needs the owner’s explicit authorisation.

1. What changed in this revision

Visual

  • Type, after screenshot 4: confident Inter Display headlines with one clause in the brand teal, Inter for reading, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. Newsreader is retired, and first-visit fonts fall from about 170 KB to about 88 KB.
  • A concrete hero: the headline now sits beside a concept panel of one class after a Mastery Check (D0), labelled as an illustration with invented data.
  • The cycle is a serpentine in the arrangement of screenshot 2: stages 1→4 across the top, 5→7 back along the bottom, and a distinct return panel pointing up to Diagnose. It has seven stages, with no eighth invented. On phones it is one column in the same order.
  • Lighter footer: the wordmark and its line, the pages, then one base row with © PT ZAYD PINTAR SELALU (ZPS), the email, Contact / Privacy / Terms and ID | EN. The language and company columns are gone.
  • Two demo invitations on the homepage: the header and the closing section. The hero keeps «Lihat cara kerjanya». Both use the one contact form, with the demo subject preselected.

Copy, both languages

  • Launch wording: 4.0 and 4.1 are presented as available. 4.5, 5.0 and beyond are «Arah ke depan / Future direction», without dates. Every former «verifikasi rilis / direncanakan» label is gone, and a check enforces that.
  • What educators see and do comes first; governance follows. Jargon is cut: «tergovernansi», «kanonik» (now «materi bersama»), «pemutar», «teaching grant». So are the awkward English phrases and «anak».
  • Each page has one job: Home introduces, Why explains the philosophy, Features gives capabilities and boundaries, Who It’s For gives benefits, Trust gives safeguards. The comparison with other LMS, library and exam platforms is removed.
  • Trust now explains each of the seven safeguards once, with one example. Separation is described as logical separation within one system. Certificate verification states what it shows and what it does not.
  • The evidence illustration says what the percentage measures (average mastery on the Mastery Check). It uses the product’s rule, a minimum sample per metric in the metric catalogue, without inventing a number, and the product’s own sentence for withheld figures.
  • Product names follow the application: Pemetaan Awal, Peta Bacaan Personal, Uji Penguasaan in Indonesian; the application’s English names Initial Mapping, Personal Reading Map, Mastery Check in English. Quiet Intelligence stays as it is.
  • Boundaries stated where a reader could blur them:
    • question reporting now, question health in 4.5;
    • professional-development participation now, growth journeys in 5.0;
    • released versions in 4.0, per-question history in 4.1;
    • an initial reading order, not continuous adaptation;
    • Content Studio capability, not a promise of ready material;
    • a demo of the parts ready to show.
  • The AI workflow now has five steps, as AIQ-01 sets out: the educator chooses a permitted source and objective; AI drafts; the drafts are checked; the educator decides; then the usual second-person review, approval and release. The retained line is «AI membantu. Pendidik memutuskan.»

Functional and privacy

  • Fixed a false «sent». A message whose delivery failed was remembered as a duplicate, so the retry was wrongly confirmed. Messages are now remembered only after delivery. A check proves the retry is really sent.
  • Deletion as it really happens. Expired security records are deleted on each use of the form and by server/cleanup.php, a daily job to schedule. The privacy policy says «deleted at the next cleanup», not an exact deadline.
  • Privacy policy corrected:
    • it now distinguishes the form (no copy kept) from the mailbox (where messages live);
    • it counts the security records and the hosting logs in the summary;
    • it calls the IP hash a pseudonym, not anonymisation, and separates it from the host’s raw logs;
    • it separates assessment provenance from personal-data retention.
  • Unchanged: spam protection, rate limits and bilingual moderation; human review of ambiguous messages; the two prescribed rejection messages.

5. Verified in this build

  • Reading order: on desktop the cycle is laid out 1→4, then 5→7 right to left, then the return under stage 1. The document order is 1–7, then the return. At 390 px it is one column in that order.
  • Language switching: on all 16 pages, both switches (header and footer) lead to the same page in the other language.
  • Contact form: delivered; double submit not resent; failed delivery reported, and the retry really sent; abuse refused with the prescribed wording in both languages and never echoed; reports flagged «PERLU DITINJAU» and delivered; honeypot; rate limit; foreign origin; header injection; works without JavaScript.
  • Footer and demo placements: no language or company column; ©, email, Contact / Privacy / Terms, ID | EN; no footer demo link; on the homepage, only the header and closing demo invitations.
  • Launch wording: no verification or planned labels anywhere; the future direction is marked; the AI workflow has five steps, including the educator’s decision and the usual approval.
  • Also covered: contrast, 14 px minimum text, 44 px controls, no overflow from 320 to 1440 px, reduced motion, no-JavaScript, nothing stored on the device, and 47 moderation cases.
  • Run: node verify/check.mjs. Every new check was proved able to fail by planting each regression it guards against.
