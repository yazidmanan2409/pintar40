Features

A sound foundation, and the right kind of help.

PINTAR 4.1: the 4.0 foundation for learning and teaching, reports and assessment, material, and accounts and institutions, plus AI help with writing questions and continuity across devices.

4.0 and 4.1 · Available4.5 and 5.0 · Future direction

AAvailable

The next step is clear.

Learners see their assignments and what to do next. Educators give material to a class, see it as learners will see it, then see the results.

  1. Initial Mapping
  2. Personal Reading Map
  3. Lessons and practice
  4. Mastery Check
  5. Result
  • The Initial Mapping and the Personal Reading Map give a starting point and an initial reading order. Continuously adapting each learner’s path is not part of 4.0.
  • Step-by-step help while learning, before the Mastery Check.
  • Classes, groups and periods kept in order, at institutional scale.
  • Professional development for educators: taking part in training programmes, kept apart from academic results. Educator growth journeys are a future direction, for 5.0.

BAvailable

What can be concluded, for each role.

Reports show mastery, hard questions and learners who may need help, to the people entitled to see them, without rankings.

Learners
Their own results, by learning objective, without rankings.
Educators
Mastery in the classes they teach, the questions most often answered wrongly, and learners who may need help.
Leadership
Patterns across the classes they are responsible for, and which figures meet the evidence minimum.
Certificate checkers
Whether a certificate was genuinely issued and is still valid, who issued it, when, and the achievement it records. Not the holder’s name or learning record.
  • Question-level reporting: how many answered correctly and which options were chosen. Judging question quality (question health) is a future direction, for 4.5.
  • Figures are held back when the data falls below that metric’s minimum sample.
  • One attempt, one official result, with the scoring rules recorded so the result can be recalculated.
  • A4-ready reports, certificates with a public authenticity check, and a Digital Card.

CAvailable

Material reviewed before it is used.

The Content Studio is where material is prepared, reviewed and released. Shared material can be used by institutions with permission, and each institution can add its own.

  • Sources recorded: the book, edition and pages, with the permission to use them.
  • Reviewed by a second person before release.
  • Released versions are kept; corrections become new versions. In 4.1 every question also has a detailed history (see below).
  • Ready-to-use material for particular subjects and levels is agreed with each institution; its availability is not promised here.

DAvailable

Accounts that follow people, not the other way round.

One person can learn, teach and manage in different places. The institution sets up the account, and each action is checked against that person’s role where they are working.

  • Accounts are created by the institution; PINTAR offers no self sign-up.
  • Class cards for learners, for learning only.
  • Class changes, moving up, school transfers and retirement without losing records that need to be kept.
  • Removed access is designed to take effect from the next request.

AI-assisted multiple-choice drafts

To save time writing questions, not to replace an educator’s judgement.

A history for every question

Building on the released versions from 4.0: every draft, revision, correction and replacement of a question is recorded, including an educator’s note on the misconception it is meant to test. Earlier results stay linked to the version used.

Continuing on another device

Start on a school computer and carry on elsewhere, without sharing a password.
