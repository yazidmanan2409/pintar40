Features

What you can do with PINTAR.

Learners work with a clear next step, educators understand the evidence, and institutions keep their material and accounts in order.

Learning and teachingReports and assessmentContent StudioAccounts and institutionsAI-assisted question draftsWhat comes next

01 · Learning and teaching

Every learner knows the next step.

Learners see their assignments and what to do next. Educators assign material, see it as learners will, then see the results.

  1. 01A clear starting point: the Initial Mapping, then a Personal Reading Map that suggests where to start reading.
  2. 02Step-by-step help while learning: a direction to think in first, then one step at a time.
  3. 03Continue on another device with a single-use code, without sharing a password. New in 4.1
  4. 04Classes, groups and periods kept in order, at any scale.

02 · Reports and assessment

Reports that help prepare the next lesson.

Educators see which objectives are strong, which questions were hard and which answers were chosen most. Leaders see patterns across the classes they are responsible for. No rankings.

  1. 01Question-level reports: how many answered correctly and which answers were chosen.
  2. 02Figures with a clear basis: when there is not yet enough data, the report says so.
  3. 03One attempt, one official result, with its scoring rules recorded.
  4. 04A4-ready reports and certificates whose authenticity can be checked.

03 · Content Studio

Material that is reviewed, and stays explainable.

In the Content Studio, material is prepared, reviewed by a second person, then released. Institutions with permission use the same shared material, and can add their own.

  • Sources recorded

    The book, edition and pages, with permission to use them.

  • Released versions kept

    A correction becomes a new version, so earlier results can still be explained.

  • A history for every question New in 4.1

    Every draft, revision and correction is recorded, including the educator’s note on the misconception it is meant to test.

04 · Accounts and institutions

Accounts that follow people, year after year.

One person can learn, teach and manage in different places with one account. The institution sets up the account and assigns the roles.

  1. 01Accounts are created by the institution, with roles assigned in each place.
  2. 02Class cards for signing in to learn, replaceable if lost.
  3. 03Class changes, moving up, school transfers and retirement without losing records that need to be kept.
  4. 04Professional-development programmes that educators take part in, kept apart from academic results.

05 · AI-assisted question drafts New in 4.1

AI assists. Educators decide.

AI drafts multiple-choice questions from the source and objective an educator chooses, to save time. The educator checks, edits or rejects them, and an accepted question is still reviewed by a second person before release.

06 · Versions and the future direction

Today, and what comes next.

PINTAR 4.1 brings together the 4.0 foundation and new help with preparing content. The future direction has no dates yet and may change.

  1. 4.0 Foundation Learning and teaching, reports and assessment, the Content Studio, and accounts and institutions. Available
  2. 4.1 Content and continuity AI-assisted question drafts, a history for every question, and continuing on another device. Available
  3. 4.5 Understand Quiet Intelligence: a few explainable observations drawn from the evidence, including judging question quality (question health). Future direction
  4. 5.0 Act and grow Educator growth journeys, learning paths that keep adapting, and interventions that are planned and then measured. Future direction
