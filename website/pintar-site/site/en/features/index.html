Foundation · 4.0

4.0 · FoundationIn release verification
A

The next step is always clear.

Learners see what to do next. Educators assign released material, preview it exactly as a learner will see it, then see the evidence.

  1. Initial mapping
  2. Personal reading map
  3. Lessons and practice
  4. Mastery test
  5. Result
  • A learner home with assignments, deadlines and the next step.
  • A teaching space for assigning material to the right class.
  • Classes, groups and periods kept in order, at institutional scale.
  • Professional development for educators in its own context, apart from academic results.
B

Prepared once. Used by many institutions.

Canonical, textbook-derived content is prepared, reviewed and released once. Each institution can add its own material without changing the original.

  • Sources recorded: the textbook, its edition and its pages.
  • A second person’s review before anything is published.
  • Preview uses the same player the learner uses.
  • A correction becomes a new release; existing results stay bound to the version used.
C

One official result. An explanation for every role.

Assessment saves answers reliably and produces one official result. Reports explain that result to each person within their authority, and never rank learners.

Learners
Their own results, by learning objective. No ranking against classmates.
Educators
Mastery in the classes they teach, the questions that proved hard, and who may need help.
Leadership
Patterns across the classes in their remit, and which figures can be interpreted yet.
Certificate checkers
A certificate’s status and issuer. Not the learning record, not even the holder’s name.
  • Answers saved reliably; a double submission still produces one result.
  • Scoring that reproduces from the same evidence and rules.
  • Answer keys are never sent to a learner’s device.
  • A4-ready reports, signed certificates with public verification, and a Digital Card.
D

One person, many roles. The record stays whole.

Accounts are set up by the institution; there is no public sign-up. Each person chooses where they are working, and every action is checked against their authority there.

  • Class cards for learners: for learning only, never for staff.
  • Class changes, moving up, school transfers and retirement without losing the record.
  • Revoked access is designed to take effect on the very next request.
  • The first administrator and their successors appointed through a guarded procedure.
