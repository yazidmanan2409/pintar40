Trust & Security

Calm on the surface. Rigorous underneath.

What people see is deliberately simple. Beneath it are seven safeguards.

In practice

Each safeguard, with one example.

  1. 01

    Identity and access

    Each action is checked against the role someone has been assigned in the place they are working.For example: A teacher who is also an administrator at another school cannot use their teaching access there.

  2. 02

    Separation between institutions

    Each institution’s data is kept logically separate within the same system, and every request is checked against its institution. Shared material carries no learner data.For example: Two schools use the same chapter; neither can see the other’s learners’ answers.

  3. 03

    Assessment integrity

    Answer keys are designed to stay off learners’ devices, and one attempt produces one official result.For example: Pressing submit twice still produces a single result.

  4. 04

    Results that can be explained

    Released material is not edited; corrections become new versions, so every result stays linked to the material and rules used at the time. How long personal data is kept is a separate matter, set in a retention policy with each institution.For example: Correcting a chapter after it has been used does not change what existing results mean.

  5. 05

    Account and institutional continuity

    Class changes, moving up a year, school transfers, retirement and the end of someone’s access are handled as deliberate actions, without erasing records that need to be kept.For example: A retiring teacher loses their teaching access, while the material they wrote stays in their name.

  6. 06

    Recorded actions

    Consequential actions record who, what, when and why.For example: When a leader opens one learner’s record, that action is recorded.

  7. 07

    Privacy

    Each person sees what their role needs, and no more.For example: The public certificate check shows whether a certificate was genuinely issued and is still valid, its issuer, issue date and achievement, without the holder’s name or learning record.

Security questions from institutions.
