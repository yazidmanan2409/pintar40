Trust & Security

Calm on the surface. Rigorous underneath.

What people see is deliberately simple. Beneath it, every request is checked, every institution is kept apart, and every consequential change is recorded.

In practice

What the protection looks like on an ordinary day.

These are PINTAR’s design principles. Each release is verified against them, and the evidence is recorded separately.

  1. 01

    Identity and access

    A role is not a permission. Every request is checked against the authority in force at that moment.A teacher who is also an administrator at another school cannot use their teaching authority there.

  2. 02

    Institutional separation

    Each institution’s data is kept apart. Shared content never carries learners’ evidence.Two schools use the same chapter; neither can see the other’s learners’ answers.

  3. 03

    Assessment integrity

    Answer keys are never sent to a learner’s device. One attempt, one official result.A submit button pressed twice still produces one result.

  4. 04

    Historical evidence

    Releases are frozen; corrections become new versions. Earlier results can still be explained.A chapter is corrected after use; existing results are still explained by the version used at the time.

  5. 05

    Account and institutional continuity

    Transfers, promotion, retirement and revoked access are handled deliberately, without erasing history.A retiring teacher loses their teaching access, but the material they wrote stays in their name.

  6. 06

    Accountable actions

    Who, what, when and why are recorded for every consequential action.A leader who opens one learner’s record does so deliberately, and the act is recorded.

  7. 07

    Privacy

    Only what is needed is shown, and only to those entitled to see it.Someone checking a certificate sees only its status and issuer, not a name or a learning record.

What we do not claim.

We list no certification or compliance standard we do not hold. If that changes, we will name it, with its number and issuer.

Nor do we publish technical security detail on a public page. We answer an institution’s security questions directly, in a demonstration or a technical conversation with your team.

Educational data inside the PINTAR application is governed by the agreement with each institution, separately from this website. Privacy Policy
