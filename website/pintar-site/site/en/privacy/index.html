3. Information we collect

Through the contact form

Your name, email address, institution or organisation (optional), subject, message and your consent. We also record the page language and the time of sending.

By the form, for security

The form does not store your IP address as it is. To limit how often messages can be sent and to recognise duplicates, it keeps a keyed hash of the address and, for messages that are sent, an unreadable fingerprint of their content. The hash is a pseudonym, not anonymisation: with the same key on the server, the same address always produces the same hash. We therefore still treat it as personal data.

For submissions that are refused, discarded as spam or flagged for review, the form keeps a security record of the time, decision, category and hashed IP address, with no name, email or message.

By the hosting provider

Separately from the form, the hosting server may keep technical access logs for every visit, including your IP address as it is, the time, the page requested and the browser type. These logs are managed by the hosting provider. [to confirm: the hosting provider, what its logs contain and how long they are kept]

What we do not collect

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising, and loads nothing from third-party services. Fonts are served from our own server.

4. Purposes and legal basis

We process form data only to read and answer your message, to arrange a demonstration if you ask for one, and to follow up partnership or support enquiries. The legal basis is the explicit consent you give by ticking the consent box (Article 20 of the UU PDP).

The hashed IP address and security records are processed to protect the form from abuse, spam and abusive content.

We do not use your data for marketing without separate consent, we do not sell it, and we make no automated decision with legal effect on you.

5. Message screening

To keep this channel professional and safe, every message is checked automatically on the server against rules on abusive language, hate speech, threats, spam and harmful links. A message that clearly breaks them is not sent, and its content is not kept; only the security record described above remains. A doubtful message is still delivered to us, marked «needs review», so that a person reads it before acting. Criticism and complaints are welcome.

7. Storage, retention and protection

  • Messages that are sent are kept in the info@pintarselalu.com mailbox for as long as needed to follow up your enquiry. The form on this website keeps no copy. [to confirm: how long email is kept, and the deletion policy]
  • Rate-limit hashes and message fingerprints stop being used after one hour, and are deleted at the next cleanup.
  • Security records stop being used after 30 days, and are deleted at the next cleanup.

Cleanup runs whenever the form is used, and in a daily scheduled cleanup on the server. A record may therefore be kept a little longer than the periods above, at most until the next cleanup. [to confirm: the daily scheduled cleanup is enabled on the production server]

Security data is kept outside the website’s public folder and is accessible only to those who administer the server. Connections to the site [to confirm: are protected by HTTPS on the production domain].

8. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device (including local storage). That is why there is no cookie banner. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential tracking, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where required.

9. Your rights

Under the UU PDP you have the right, among others, to:

  • be informed about how your data is processed;
  • complete, update or correct inaccurate data;
  • access your data and obtain a copy;
  • have processing ended, and your data deleted or destroyed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • have processing suspended or restricted;
  • object to decisions based solely on automated processing; and
  • bring a claim and receive compensation for a breach in the processing of your personal data.

To exercise these rights, email info@pintarselalu.com with the subject “Personal data request”. We may ask for information to confirm your identity, and will respond within the time set by law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before.

10. Children

This website and its form are intended for educational institutions and adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us.
