# PINTAR 4.0: entry guide for the owner's walkthrough

This is not a script. It says only where to go and who you can be; what to do there is yours to discover.

**Where:** `https://staging.pintarselalu.com`, once the owner has authorized promotion of the verified candidate. The exact commit staging serves is stated in the promotion report; until then, staging still runs `c4944fab`, without the latest corrections.

**The school:** «Sekolah Uji P7B 1107», a synthetic school. Everything in it is synthetic: people, classes and results. Nothing you do there reaches a real school or a real person.

**Who you can be** (one sign-in each; «Keluar» ends it):

| Role | What it is |
|---|---|
| Admin sekolah | Runs the school: structure, people, roles, accounts, school settings |
| Guru | Teaches a class; prepares material in Studio |
| Kepala departemen | Reviews and approves material; reads the department's reports |
| Dekan / pimpinan | Reads the school's reports |
| Murid | Signs in with a class card and PIN |

**Signing in:** staff sign in with their email at «Masuk»; the sign-in code arrives in the test mailbox. Learners sign in with a class card number and PIN. **The addresses, the mailbox and the card details are given to you separately, never in this repository.**

**If something stops you,** «Laporkan masalah» (in the menu) records it with the page you were on. That is the feedback route we want you to use.

**Not part of this walkthrough:** production, real schools and Dr Gladys's team.
