# PINTAR roadmap · owner decisions of 11 November 2026

**Authority:** the owner, 11 Nov 2026, after the Chief Architect's Strategic Architectural Outlook, the roadmap
reconciliation and an independent assessment. Recorded here by reference.

## 1 · Roadmap authority

- **`docs/roadmap/PINTAR-ROADMAP.md` is the one authoritative statement of PINTAR's direction after 4.1.**
- **It supersedes the roadmap authority that D-144 (2 Nov, `docs/coordination/DECISIONS.md`) gave to the September
  documents:** `docs/reports/ROADMAP-STATUS-2026-09-29.md`, `docs/reports/ARCHITECTURE-HORIZON-REVIEW-2026-09-29.md`
  and `docs/reports/PINTAR-4.1-LOCAL-EXPERIENCE-LAYER-FEASIBILITY-STUDY-2026-10-02.md`. D-144's other provisions are
  not changed by this record.
- The September Horizon Review is preserved as historical evidence. Its roadmap direction is superseded; its technical
  findings remain available for investigation. Nothing in it is deleted or rewritten.
- The roadmap is approved as strategic direction. **Its implementation remains subject to separate planning and
  authorization.** Nothing here authorizes 4.5 or 5.0 engineering, and nothing here changes PINTAR 4.1 or its plan.

## 2 · The direction

- **PINTAR 4.5 — UNDERSTAND.** Transform trustworthy educational evidence into meaningful, explainable, appropriately
  governed educational intelligence.
- **PINTAR 5.0 — ACT & GROW.** Help educators and institutions act on that intelligence, pursue improvement and examine
  whether their actions produce meaningful results.
- **Beyond 5.0.** Explore longer-term educational intelligence, research and platform capabilities without committing
  to them prematurely.
