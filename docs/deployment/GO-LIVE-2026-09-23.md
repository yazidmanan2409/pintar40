## Before Gladys's first working session (checked 2026-09-23)

- **She can use both demo schools.** Evaluated as her account, in a
  read-only transaction that was rolled back: in `sd-zps` and `sma-zps` she
  holds CAN_DRAFT, CAN_REVIEW, CAN_FREEZE_RELEASE, CAN_PUBLISH,
  CAN_ASSIGN_LEARNING, CAN_QUICK_ASSESSMENT and CAN_PROVISION_PEOPLE.
- **`/aktivasi` admits a SYSTEM_ADMIN.** So she can create each school's
  first administrator herself.
- **Nobody publishes their own lesson alone.** The content graph is
  IN_REVIEW → APPROVED → FROZEN → PUBLISHED, and `enforce_content_status`
  refuses "the reviewer may not be the author". A lesson Gladys drafts must be
  approved by someone else who holds CAN_REVIEW in that school.
  INSTITUTION_ADMIN and HEAD_OF_DEPARTMENT both hold it; EDUCATOR does not.
  So the school administrator she creates is the natural second pair of
  eyes.
- **Storage is ready.** The `assets` bucket is private with a 100 MB per-file
  limit, and uploads go through the service-role key validated on 2026-09-23.

## How Gladys signs in

1. Open https://app.pintarselalu.com/masuk and enter her email address.
2. Open the sign-in link from that email on the **same device and browser**
   that asked for it.

From there, onboarding follows runbook §4 step 5:

1. create the institution and its academic period;
2. grant INSTITUTION_ADMIN at the institution's **root** node. A person who
   holds nothing yet appears under "Holds nothing yet" in the person selector.
