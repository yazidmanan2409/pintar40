## 4 · Certificates: how Dr Gladys's guidance has been applied

**Already true in PINTAR (no change needed):**
- **The passing conditions are fixed before the assessment.** The school's pass mark is recorded on each attempt when
  it starts. No mark, no certificate.
- **A certificate also requires** the mandatory questions answered correctly, official evidence, and the result in
  force.
- **No score is printed** on the certificate or shown on the public verification page.
- **Corrections are governed:** a corrected answer key triggers a review of affected certificates. A reissued
  certificate replaces the old one, which then reads «digantikan».
- **The feedback is narrative and based on evidence:** whether the target was met, which sections to re-read (with
  links to the lessons), why each answer was right or wrong, and «belum cukup data» when the evidence is thin. No AI
  writes any of it.
- **Retakes follow each institution's own rules,** with approval by a second educator, and every attempt is kept on
  record.

**Approved changes, made after Z and before the first real certificate:**
1. The title becomes **«SERTIFIKAT»**.
2. The achievement line reads: *«Telah dinyatakan memenuhi kriteria kelulusan yang ditetapkan berdasarkan hasil
   asesmen pada Materi Pembelajaran: [nama materi].»*
3. **«Predikat» is removed** from the certificate and the public verification page.
4. **Thinking prompts, hints and worked examples are switched off in certifying assessments only.** They remain in
   lessons and practice.
5. **Reading the lessons before Uji Penguasaan is advised, not required,** and the screen will say so.
6. **The attempt number** appears in the learner's result and staff views only, never on the certificate.

**Decided by Yazid:**
- **Certification:** stays on the overall pass mark plus the mandatory questions. It does not require every learning
  objective to be mastered individually.
- **Accessibility:** extra time remains. Any further accommodation goes to a separate educational review.
- **Terminology** (Peserta Didik, Pendidik, Institusi, Pimpinan, Materi Pembelajaran): recorded as guidance for the
  future. No renaming in 4.1.

**Still open:** whether a retake may repeat the same questions. It will be checked first, then brought back.

## 6 · The first pilot: 10 adults (planning only)

- **Who:** one group of 10 adults, no children. Each takes a staff role and also sits colleagues' assessments.
- **Duration:** three weeks, using existing features only.
- **Purpose:** to see whether ordinary educators can use PINTAR for something worthwhile **without needing the
  development team.**
- **What it learns:** whether participants can get started, complete one full teaching cycle, and understand their
  results; where they need help; and whether everything runs cleanly.
- **Certificates:** none issued during the pilot.
- **Timing:** it starts only after 4.1 is accepted and the readiness and legal steps are done. Each launch step needs
  Yazid's approval.
