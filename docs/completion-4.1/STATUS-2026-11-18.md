## 6 · C3w: what AI-assisted drafting will be

The principle is **"AI drafts. Educators decide."**

- A teacher can ask for draft questions from their own lesson material. Every draft must be accepted, edited or deleted
  by a teacher, and approved by a second educator, before any learner sees it. **The database enforces this, not only
  the screen.**
- **Learner privacy:** no learner's name, ID or results is ever sent to an AI provider. Material that contains them is
  refused before anything is sent.
- **Spending safety:** each school has an allowance, set by PINTAR, with a ceiling per provider and one for the whole
  platform. Every request reserves its worst-case cost before it is sent, so limits can't be overspent, even by two
  requests at the same moment.
- **Provider choice (ruling 40):** schools will choose from providers PINTAR has qualified. Provider names appear to
  staff only, never to learners. Learners see ordinary approved questions, with no AI label.
- **The evaluation (E) is the qualification evidence** for which providers can be offered.

The design is reviewed and approved by Sharon (security and data) and Daisy (wording and screens).
