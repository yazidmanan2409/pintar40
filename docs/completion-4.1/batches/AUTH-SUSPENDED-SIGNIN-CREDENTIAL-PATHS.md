## Notes
1. Three paths should get the one-line `orang_ditangguhkan` refusal: the two permits (in progress), `recovery_grant_issue` (defence in depth; one check covers the email and PIN grants), and `authorize_account_activation` (or `handle_new_auth_user`). The last is the only one where the lock is actually bypassed at the Auth level today.
2. Every self-service path that runs on the person's own JWT (wizard, set_my_pin, claim_username, complete_my_setup, sesi_periksa) is stopped twice: Auth (sessions deleted at M/20261122100000:84, then sign-in and verifyOtp refused) and PRE M/20270113130000:499-503.
3. Service-role paths skip PRE, because PRE returns early for any role other than `authenticated` (M/20270113130000:485-487). Only Auth's refusal of the session, or of the OTP verify, guards them. That is why the permits and grants need their own check.
4. Admin writes through `auth.admin.updateUserById` (card reissue, conversion, email correction, recovery password) never clear `banned_until`. Only `aktifkan_kembali_akun` (M/20270113130000:142→155) and `buka_kembali_akun` (:343→365), both platform admin only, unlock an account. These are the intended unlockers, not defects.
5. `authorize_new_person` (M/20261111100000:489; S/app/api/akun/aktivasi/route.ts:61-66) always creates a new person, which cannot already be locked. It is left out of the table.
6. Assumption not checked against GoTrue's source: that Auth's verifyOtp refuses a banned user with `user_banned`. The code relies on this at S/lib/aktivasi.ts:170 and S/lib/pemulihan.ts:146.
