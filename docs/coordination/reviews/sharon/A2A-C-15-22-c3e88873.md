## C17-1 · BLOCKER: "EXPIRED inside its grace at `T_stop`" is judged on two deadlines

- **Protection** (`pengerjaan_dilindungi`) uses `s.closure_at <= t_stop` and `t_stop <= s.closure_deadline + grace`.
- **Marking** (`pengerjaan_tandai_darurat`, and the lift's `pengerjaan_tandai_saat_angkat`) uses `s.authoritative_deadline is not null` and `sejak <= s.authoritative_deadline + grace`.
- **Take a window-timed sitting** (`authoritative_deadline` is null) that expired just before the stop and was inside its grace at `T_stop`:
  - it is **protected**, so the decider skips it and its late answers are refused during the stop;
  - but it is **never marked**, so it is released with no shift.
  - After the lift its grace, counted from `closes_at` with no shift, has run out during the stop. Answers the learner sent in it are lost.
- **Required:**
  - one helper, `pengerjaan_dalam_tenggang_pada(s, t)`, on one basis: the closing time recorded at expiry, `closure_deadline`, plus the definition's grace;
  - protection, the batch marking and the lift's marking all call it;
  - a window-timed EXPIRED sitting is marked like any other and shifted through `darurat_geser_detik`;
  - the late-sync and finalize readers of the grace add the shift (they are A2A-T01's HARUS).
- **Test:** a window-timed sitting EXPIRED inside its grace at `T_stop` is marked. After the lift, a late answer inside the shifted grace is accepted and finalize waits for it. Add a plant that restores the `authoritative_deadline` basis in marking.
- Could reasonably have been anticipated: yes (Q1, readers). Two readers of one fact used two columns.

## C18-1 · BLOCKER: the lift's marking is unbounded, under the exclusive fence

- `pengerjaan_tandai_saat_angkat` runs inside the lift, under the exclusive fence and inside the RPC's 8 s.
- It joins **every** sitting created before `T_stop`, platform-wide, with `darurat_sejak is null`. It then evaluates `maintenance_affects_pada` per row.
- If the batch marking has fallen behind, which is the case this function exists for, it marks the whole remainder in one statement.
- Meanwhile:
  - every protected decision on the platform waits on the fence, and is refused after 2 s with `SITTING_FENCE_BUSY`;
  - a lift over 8 s is rolled back. The stop stays in force (the safe direction), but **the lift cannot complete at scale**, which is a failed recovery.
- **Required:**
  - drive it from the window's people: memberships at `node_ids` valid at `T_stop`, roles scoped there, or the school or platform. Do not scan every sitting;
  - use an index on open sittings by person (a partial index on the open statuses);
  - **scenario 8 adds the worst case:** 50,000 affected sittings, **no batch marking run before the lift**. The lift's work under the fence is still ≤ 0.5 s (§3).
- **If that does not hold,** the lift must not mark. Instead, the first touch and the release batch credit an unmarked sitting whose window has lifted, if it was protected at that window's `T_stop`, recording the window as credited so the shift is applied once. Then bring the design back to me.
- Could reasonably have been anticipated: yes (Q3 and the 8 s constraint). Work was added under the exclusive fence without its arithmetic.
