## C39-1 · BLOCKER: the departure must apply at first touch, not only in the batch

- After the lift, a sitting with `darurat_keluar` is no longer protected. Until the next release batch, which runs every five minutes or later, it is an ordinary open sitting.
- **Any act on it in that gap is judged as if no departure had happened:**
  - the departed learner answers and hands in (`record_answer`, `finish_my_session` → finalize);
  - or a teacher acts on the sitting.
- The sitting is then **scored**, where the ordinary rule, applied at departure, would have ended it, except where its deadline had passed.
- **That changes O1's outcome** ("the departure's ordinary rule is applied after the lift"), and makes it depend on the batch's timing. It is the same shape as C7-2, the release, and C18-1, the credit: each is applied at first touch.
- **Required:**
  - `pengerjaan_pagar`, when not protected, after the release and the credit, applies the departure to a sitting with `darurat_keluar` that is still open, before the caller's own act.
    - Split `pengerjaan_keluar_sesudah_henti` into an inner step without its own call to `pengerjaan_pagar`, so it is not re-entered.
    - The caller's act then sees the terminated sitting. A learner's answer is refused as for any ended sitting.
  - The batch stays, for sittings nobody touches.
- **Test:** a departure during a stop, then the lift. Before any batch run, the learner answers and hands in. The answer is refused; the sitting is TERMINATED `INSTITUTIONAL_REVOCATION` by the departure's actor, and audited once. Add a plant: no first-touch application, so the hand-in is scored.
- Could reasonably have been anticipated: yes (Q2). Every other after-the-lift rule in C is applied at first touch.
