## Item 8 · the last school administrator

**(a) Z, agreed.**
- It is small, red first, and the same rule the platform role already has. My §16 finding 5 stopped the last PINTAR administrator closing their own account; a school deserves the same.
- The harm is recoverable, because a platform admin can appoint a new one. But until then the school cannot administer itself:
  - no roles;
  - no pass mark;
  - no transfers.

**(b) Count only an appointment valid now.** A future-dated or pending appointment leaves the school without an administrator in the meantime, and may never begin. The test is a `role_assignments` row with:
- role INSTITUTION_ADMIN, scoped to that school;
- `revoked_at is null`;
- `valid_from <= now() < valid_until`.

**(c) Yes: the other administrator must be usable now.**
- **Not usable:**
  - a person suspended (`orang_ditangguhkan`);
  - a person anonymised;
  - a person with no sign-in account;
  - a person whose sign-in is locked (`akun_kunci`, or `auth.users.banned_until` in the future).
- **The caller does not count.** Check every school the caller administers. The refusal names one code, and does not say which school, beyond the caller's own.

**(d) A second route to the same lockout. Please include it in the same migration.**
- `revoke_role(p_assignment, p_reason)` (`20261111100000`) lets a school's INSTITUTION_ADMIN revoke any INSTITUTION_ADMIN assignment in their school, including their own. So the last one can be revoked, or two administrators can revoke each other.
- Apply the same guard when the revoked assignment is INSTITUTION_ADMIN and the caller is not a platform admin. A platform admin's revocation stays allowed, as a deliberate act, like `tangguhkan_akun`.

**(e) Concurrency.** Two administrators closing or revoking at the same moment can each see the other as usable.
- Take `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('institution_admin:' || institution_id, 0))` for each school, in institution-id order.
  - In `minta_akun`, after the caller's `persons` row lock, which it already takes first.
  - In `revoke_role`, before the check.
- Red cases:
  - the last administrator closes and is refused;
  - the last administrator revokes their own role and is refused;
  - with two administrators where the other is suspended or locked, closing is refused;
  - with two usable administrators, closing is allowed;
  - a platform admin's revocation of the last one is allowed.

**Could reasonably have been anticipated:** the `revoke_role` route is the same class as the one I raised for the platform role in §16. I should have looked for the school-level twin then.
