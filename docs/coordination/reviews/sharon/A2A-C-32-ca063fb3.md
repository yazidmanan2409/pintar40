## C30-2: closed. The rule is as ruled (and the 07:40 addendum: a composition, not new behaviour)

**At a hold during a stop:**
- `pengerjaan_tahan_orang` measures the time left at `T_stop` of the chain in force (`penghentian_berlaku`), not at the hold.

**While held:**
- no pending credit (`penghentian_kredit` returns 0 while frozen);
- the credit write is skipped, never raised, so `SITTING_FROZEN` no longer blocks a touch;
- no new mark;
- a release clears the mark with no shift, and moves the watermark past the chain.

**At the resume:**
- `lanjutkan_pengerjaan`, the only unfreezing writer (I read every `unfrozen_at =` on the branch), moves the watermark past every chain lifted by then. Nothing spent under the hold is credited afterwards.
