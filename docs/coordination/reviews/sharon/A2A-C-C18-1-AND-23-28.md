## 1 · Is the design correct, and does it preserve D-1?

**The design:**
- the lift records `t_lift` only;
- an unmarked sitting of a lifted window is credited once, by its watermark `darurat_dikredit_sampai`, at first touch or in the release batch;
- marking before the lift stays as it is.

**D1 · required: the pending credit is part of the effective deadline, not only a write.**
- Between the lift and the first touch, an unmarked sitting is neither protected nor credited. Every **read-only** HARUS reader would then show or decide on the **unshifted** deadline:
  - `session_state`, the player's countdown;
  - `may_continue_session`, which decides whether the learner may go on;
  - `pengerjaan_saya`;
  - `daftar_pengerjaan`, and through it AturPengerjaan's KLIEN computation;
  - `maintenance_draining`.
- A learner could be told their time is up when it is not.
- **Required:**
  - `pengerjaan_tenggat_efektif` adds the **pending** credit itself: for a window `w` with `t_stop >= watermark`, the same rule as the credit, and the union chain;
  - every HARUS reader already reads through it, or adds `darurat_geser_detik` (A2A-C708 enforces that);
  - the first touch and the batch then only **persist** what every reader already sees;
  - the pending computation reads only lifted EMERGENCY windows whose `t_stop` is at or after the sitting's watermark. That is a small, indexed read of `maintenance_windows`.

**With D1, each case:**
- **Late marking (A2A-K01):**
  - while the window is in force, protection does not need a mark (affected at `T_stop`, open then);
  - after the lift, the credit or the pending credit gives exactly `t_lift − t_stop`.
- **Interrupted marking, or no batch at all:** the same. A marked sitting is released by the mark, an unmarked one is credited, and both use the same chain and the same watermark.
- **A learner who never touches the sitting again:**
  - the release batch credits it;
  - until then, every reader already sees the shifted deadline (D1);
  - if neither runs, the closer selects it only by the shifted deadline. The closer's selection reads `pengerjaan_tenggat_efektif`, or, after D1, the same pending credit.
- **The closer reaching an unmarked lifted sitting first:** `pengerjaan_tutup_satu` calls `pengerjaan_pagar`, which credits first, before it re-reads and locks the sitting. The decider then judges the credited deadline. Correct.
- **Status "at `T_stop`" is safe to read from the current row:**
  - a sitting protected during the stop cannot change status;
  - a sitting made after `T_stop` is excluded;
  - a write after the lift passes through `pengerjaan_pagar` first, as A2A-C702 enforces, so it is credited before any status change.

**Required for the credit write:**
- the sitting is locked `FOR UPDATE` and its watermark re-read under that lock, so that two touches, or a touch and the batch, credit once;
- the batch uses `SKIP LOCKED`;
- tests: two concurrent first touches credit once; and the plant "no watermark", in which a second touch credits twice.

## 2 · Does the lift stay within 0.5 s and 8 s?

- **Yes.** Under the exclusive fence the lift writes the window row, its notices and its audit row only. That is §3's budget: 3 × 1.5 + 2 × 0.1 + 0.5 = 5.2 s, under 8 s.
- The 50,000-row write, 5.5 s through the triggers (commit 29's measurement), moves to the release batch. That batch runs in bounded committed batches **with no fence**, so no decision waits on it.
- **Scenario 8 must show:**
  - the lift's work under the fence ≤ 0.5 s, with no batch run before the lift;
  - the release batch's throughput;
  - the pending-credit read's cost per call, from D1, in the hook's or the player's path.

## 3 · Is anything a change to approved behaviour?

- **No.** §3 as approved says the lift's work under the fence is "the window's end, the audit row: 0.5 s; the marking runs after, in its own batches (D6.7)".
- Marking at the lift (commit 18) was the deviation. This design returns to the approved one.
- D-1 is preserved, with D1.
- **Visible behaviour:** with D1, nobody sees an unshifted deadline after a lift. **Without D1, a learner could briefly see time up after a lift. That would be a visible change, and I do not accept it.**
- Nothing for the owner, provided D1 is built.
