## 1 · Account lifecycle: the School Administrator journey

### 1.1 Where an administrator looks first (§8.1)

**They look for the person, not a section.**
- «Pak Yusuf pensiun» starts with Pak Yusuf; «Ani lulus» starts with Ani, or with her class.
- Konfigurasi today is organised by **task** («Pindah kelas», «Perpindahan murid», «Peran & akses», «Pergantian tahun ajaran»). None of them is «find this person and see what is open for them».
- «Peran & akses» is the nearest for staff, but it is a list of **grants**, one role at a time. Revoking each role with its own free-text reason does not read as «he has left».

**Correction:**
- Put each action **on the person's row in the list the administrator already uses for that group**.
- Add **one search** (name or registration number) at the top of each of the two groups, «Murid» and «Staf dan akses».

That is no new page: it reuses the row-and-row-action grammar of «Pindah kelas» (one primary per row, D-140) and «Peran & akses».

### 1.2 The three actions

**A. «Akhiri pendaftaran» (a learner) · Konfigurasi › Murid › «Murid di kelas»**

- **Place.** The «Pindah kelas» section already lists a class's students («Tampilkan murid») with «Pindahkan» per row. Rename it **«Murid di kelas»**, and give each student row two actions:
  - «Pindahkan» (unchanged);
  - «Akhiri pendaftaran» (quiet, red text, the `pin-bahaya` opener, since it ends something).

  «Murid yang belum punya kelas» stays in this section, as now.
- **Within PINTAR, point elsewhere.** When the reason is a move to another school *in PINTAR*, the form says so and sends the administrator to «Perpindahan murid», so that the history follows the student:

  > «Pindah ke sekolah lain yang memakai PINTAR? Pakai **Perpindahan murid**, supaya riwayatnya ikut.»

**B. «Akhiri di sekolah ini» (an educator) · Konfigurasi › Staf dan akses › «Staf sekolah»**

- **Place.** A list of people with any live role at this school, one row per **person**, showing their roles («Guru 5B, Guru 5C · Kepala departemen Matematika»).
- **Row actions:** «Ubah peran» (opens today's «Peran & akses» for that person) and **«Akhiri di sekolah ini»** (the `pin-bahaya` opener).
- **The two verbs stay apart:**
  - «Cabut» stays inside «Peran & akses» for **changing duties** (no longer Guru 5C, still a teacher here);
  - «Akhiri di sekolah ini» means **he has left**.

**C. «Daftarkan kembali» (a returning learner) · Konfigurasi › Murid › «Murid yang sudah keluar»**

- **Place.** A new list in the same group, with the same search: «Ani Lestari · SDC000123 · Berakhir 20 Jun 2026 · Lulus». One row action, «Daftarkan kembali».
- **What it asks:**
  - the **Kelas** (this year's learners' Kelas only, as in «Murid yang belum punya kelas»);
  - the reason;
  - the date.
- **A returning educator** is not a separate action. «Staf yang sudah berakhir» lists them with «Berikan peran lagi», which opens «Peran & akses» with the person already chosen (R5). It is the same person, so never a new import.

### 1.3 Reason lists (§4), corrected

### 1.4 The effective date, and the one confirmation

**The date field:**
- **Label:** «**Tanggal kejadian**», with the hint «Hari ini atau sebelumnya. Akses berakhir saat Anda menyimpan, apa pun tanggal yang dipilih.»
- **The default is today.** A future date cannot be picked; the date control's maximum is today.
- **No refusal message for a future date.** The control does not offer one, so it never has to say no.
- **English:** «Date it happened» / «Today or earlier. Access ends when you save, whatever date you choose.»

**A past date before later activity.** If the date is before the person's last recorded activity at the school, a neutral note shows before confirming. It is neither a block nor an error:

> «Ani masih mengerjakan asesmen pada 5 Jun 2026, sesudah tanggal ini. Hasil itu tetap tersimpan.»

**→ Sharon:** what such evidence means for reports (§7.7 of the inventory).

**One confirmation per action.** It uses the `KonfirmasiBahaya` grammar: red opener, «Batal» quiet and focused, red confirm. It names the **person, the school and what ends**, then what does **not** end:

> Mulai sekarang Ani tidak dapat lagi mengerjakan asesmen atau menerima materi di sekolah ini. Hasil, sertifikat dan riwayatnya tetap tersimpan, dan tetap dapat ia baca. Akun PINTAR-nya tidak dinonaktifkan.
> Lulus · tanggal kejadian 20 Jun 2026
> [Batal] [**Ya, akhiri pendaftaran**]

> **Akhiri Pak Yusuf Hidayat di SD Cendekia Nusantara?**

Paket, tinjauan dan keputusan yang pernah ia buat tetap tercatat atas namanya. Akun PINTAR-nya tetap ada, termasuk perannya di sekolah lain bila ada.

## 2 · The integrity page's count line (SI-D01)

**Wording: change it.**
1. **«bagian» is the wrong word.** «Bagian» is a structural level in PINTAR (Bagian · Tingkat · Kelas, the frozen terminology). Lowercase «bagian lain sekolah» reads as «another Bagian», which is not what is hidden. What the owner ruled on is **other departments**, and the page's readers are Kepala departemen.
2. **«peninjau bagian itu» names no role.** The role is the Kepala departemen.
3. **The empty state would contradict it.** Under the line, «Tidak ada catatan yang menunggu tinjauan.» would then sit under «3 catatan menunggu». The empty state must say *yours*.

| n | ID | EN |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 2 | «Di departemen lain, **{n} catatan** menunggu tinjauan Kepala departemennya.» | «In other departments, **{n} notes** are waiting for their head of department's review.» |
| 1 | «Di departemen lain, **1 catatan** menunggu tinjauan Kepala departemennya.» | «In another department, **1 note** is waiting for its head of department's review.» |
| 0 | «Di departemen lain, tidak ada catatan yang menunggu.» | «No notes are waiting in other departments.» |
| no other department (reader's scope is the whole school, or the school has one department) | nothing | nothing |

**Show n = 0** when other departments exist: the owner's ruling is about oversight, and «nothing waiting elsewhere» is oversight too. Hiding it makes «none» indistinguishable from «you cannot see». This needs the RPC to say whether other departments exist, not only rows with `waiting > 0`. **→ Sharon / engineering.**

**The empty state:** `studio.integritas.kosong` → «Tidak ada catatan yang menunggu tinjauan **Anda**.» / «No note is waiting for **your** review.»

**Place.**
- **Not inside «Menunggu tinjauan».** That section is the reader's own work list, with actions; a count of others' cases there reads as part of it.
- **Put the line directly under the page's explanation** («Catatan ini bahan pertimbangan manusia, bukan kesimpulan tentang murid.»), as a second `pin-ket`, before «Menunggu tinjauan».
- **This reads as a page-level overview,** and a screen-reader user hears it before their own list, not between the heading and the cases.
- **No link, no number styling as a figure:** it is a sentence, so no one tries to open the other departments' cases.

## 3 · C3 evaluation prompt, v1 → v2

**Overall:** clear, well ordered and short. The draft status, «Pakai hanya bahan ini» and rule 7 are exactly right. Five changes, so this becomes **v2**.

1. **«Sapa murid dengan «kalian» bila perlu» → no direct address.** The owner ruled on 9 Nov that PINTAR keeps «Anda» throughout, SD learners included. «Kalian» would put the opposite register into the very items being compared. «Anda» in SD mathematics items reads stiff, and the question is still open for content (my 4.1 consistency item 4). So the fair instruction is neutral, and SD items rarely need direct address anyway («Rina mengumpulkan…»). It also keeps the language rating about the language, not about a register the provider was told to use.
2. **The schema must be in the text.** «Jawab hanya dengan JSON yang sesuai skema yang diberikan»: the schema is given only to providers with a structured-output feature (README:62). The others receive a reference to a schema they never see, so the conditions are not identical, and the difference would show up in the language results as noise. One sentence listing the fields, identical for everyone, fixes it. **→ Sharon** (comparability).
3. **Terminology, aligned with Studio:**
   - «pilihan salah» → «**pengecoh** (pilihan salah)». Studio's own word: «Tidak satu pun pengecoh tertaut ke miskonsepsi».
   - «miskonsepsi» is right for an authoring task (Studio uses it); the reports' «pemahaman keliru» is the reader's word.
   - «kunci jawaban» is fine in a teacher-facing instruction.
4. **Indonesian number and money formats.** Without saying so, some providers will write «2.5» and «Rp15,000». PINTAR's content uses «2,5» and «Rp15.000». This matters to a language rating.
5. **Names and contexts:** one clause asking for everyday Indonesian contexts, with varied names. It costs nothing, and it is what a reviewer will otherwise flag.

**Small:**
- «murid kelas 5 SD» → «murid **Kelas 5** SD», the capitalisation used everywhere else.
- **Difficulty** («Mudah / Sedang / Sulit») is not asked for. That is fine for this evaluation; record it as a known difference from a real Studio draft.
