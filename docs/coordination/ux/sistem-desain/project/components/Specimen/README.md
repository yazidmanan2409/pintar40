PINTAR at a glance: every family of control together, so the whole system can be judged on one screen.

**Status: a review sheet, not a component.** Nothing here is new. It puts the families from the other cards side by side, the way the 4 Oct references present a system, so Yazid can ask in one look whether it feels like PINTAR.

**What to look for**
- **Calm:** one green action per screen, neutral everything else, colour only with a word.
- **Consistency:** controls are 44 px tall with 8 px corners, small labels are pills, panels have 12 px corners, and lines are 1 px.
- **Structure:** hierarchy comes from type and space, and a selected state is filled or underlined, never hinted at.

The panels and their white ground are review chrome for this sheet. In the product, a panel must earn its place (see Panel).
