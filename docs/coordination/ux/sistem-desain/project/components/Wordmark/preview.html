Wordmark PINTAR dan ruang kosongnya

XPINTAR

X is the cap height. The clear space is X on every side (the dashed line), and nothing enters it. In the product's own navigation and top bar, at least ½ X.

Not a logo: the tab icon, drawn at size

A functional identifier, not a second logo: a solid PINTAR-green square with nothing on it, no letter, no P, no symbol. Rounded in the tab, full bleed where the phone rounds it. A permanent app-icon design is a later decision.

A proof, until the master is made

The product uses outlined artwork made from Helvetica Neue 65 Medium (tracking +50, metric kerning, one optical pass), never live text. The master is made only after the font licence is verified to permit it. This page has neither the font nor the master, so each wordmark here is set in the viewer's own face: Helvetica Neue on Apple devices, a stand-in elsewhere.
