Three content widths, one gutter rule and four screen bands; no 12-column grid.

**Status: CHANGED, APPROVED** (the bands at 640, 1024 and 1280, question 1, 5 Oct); the widths are kept.

**Widths.** `measure-narrow` 560 for a task, `measure-read` 760 for reading and forms, `measure-stage` 1120 for tables and overviews.

**Bands.** Small under 640 (top bar and drawer, 16 px gutter); medium 640 to 1023 (top bar and drawer); large 1024 to 1279 (rail); wide 1280 and up (expanded navigation).

**Rules**
- A page is one column at its measure. Side-by-side items use an auto-fit grid with a minimum width, so the number of columns follows the screen.
- Components respond to their container, not the screen.
- The reference staff screen is the 1366 × 768 school laptop. A phone is never required for a student's school journey.
