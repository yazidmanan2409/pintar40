«PINTAR terkunci»: on a shared computer, after five idle minutes, the PIN brings the person back to where they were.

**Status: NEW** (Gate 6, BOB-29 and DAISY-23, not built yet). Its words are sample text from IDENTITY-4.0 §7 and the board.

**Anatomy.** The mark, the title «PINTAR terkunci», one sentence saying why («Komputer ini tidak dipakai selama 5 menit»), the PIN field (4 digits, 52 px, with «Tampilkan PIN»), «Buka PINTAR» (primary, full width), then «Bukan akun Anda?» and «Keluar».

**Rules**
- No name on the lock: it is on a shared screen. «Keluar» is always on it.
- A wrong PIN says how many tries remain and what happens after the last («Sisa 1 kali percobaan; setelah itu sesi ini berakhir…»). Three wrong end that session (IDENTITY-4.0 §3).
- The lock hides the page underneath completely; nothing of the work shows through.
- Built on the PIN field (PinInput) and the action grammar (TombolKirim's «Memeriksa…»).
