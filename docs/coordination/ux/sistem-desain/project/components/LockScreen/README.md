«PINTAR terkunci»: on a shared computer, after five idle minutes, the PIN brings the person back to where they were.

**Status: NEW** (Gate 6, built: BOB-29, D-101). Its words are the copy table's (`kunci.*`).

**Anatomy.** The wordmark, the title «PINTAR terkunci», «Terkunci untuk» and the staff member's name, one sentence saying why (`kunci.lede`), the PIN field («PIN pribadi (4 angka)», 52 px, with «Tampilkan PIN»), «Lanjutkan» (primary, full width), «Lupa PIN?», then «Bukan akun Anda?» and «Keluar».

**Rules**
- A staff member's lock names its owner, so the next person sees whose session it is. A learner's class card names no one and asks for «PIN kartu». «Keluar» is always on it.
- A wrong PIN says how many tries remain («Anda bisa mencoba 2 kali lagi.») and, at the last one, what happens next (`kunci.tidak_cocok.1`). Three wrong end that session.
- The lock hides the page underneath completely; nothing of the work shows through.
- Built on the PIN field (PinInput) and the action grammar (TombolKirim's «Memeriksa…»).
