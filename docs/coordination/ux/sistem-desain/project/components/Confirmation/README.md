Set it up, see what will happen, then do it, in the page: the confirmation takes the place of the action area.

**Status: KEPT** (HalamanKomitmen, TahapKomitmen), restyled; the destructive form is written down.

**Anatomy.** A question as the title («Jadikan admin sekolah?», after «Admin sekolah» was pressed on Peran & akses, D-101(2)), who and where it applies to, the consequences in plain words (what changes, what is recorded, what can be undone), and two actions: the act named on its button, and the way back.

**Destructive.** The panel's edge and the confirm button are `status-blocked`; the confirm button names the act («Nonaktifkan Kelas 5C», never «Ya»); focus starts on «Batal».

**Rules**
- Scope: a Confirmation follows the act being pressed. A review that comes before the one act (Siapkan's «Tinjauan sebelum disediakan», an assessment's «Kumpulkan» panel) is a Panel holding the act; its way back is the page's back link, and it needs no second «Batal».
- A consequential act that needs a reason (revoking a certificate, correcting an email or a name) is a form page with this in-page confirmation, never a Dialog: the Dialog asks a short question and holds no form.
- Say what stays safe («Jawaban dan hasilnya tetap tersimpan.»).
- Looking never commits: opening the confirmation changes nothing.
- No typed confirmation unless the act is irreversible and large; PINTAR has none today.
