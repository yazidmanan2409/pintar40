«Komputer siapa ini?»: asked once, after signing in on a browser PINTAR has not seen, with two whole-row choices and the safer one chosen.

**Status: NEW** (Gate 6). Sample text from IDENTITY-4.0 C4, C11 and §7.

**Anatomy.** A title that is the question, one sentence of why, two whole-row choices (`pds-pilihan`), each with what it means for the person, and «Lanjut».

**Rules**
- The shared computer is first and chosen: it is the safer answer and the normal case at school (D-099).
- Each choice says its consequence in plain words (5 minutes to the lock and 60 to sign-out; about 7 days on a personal device).
- The whole row is the target; the radio is only the mark.
- Asked once per browser; never again on every sign-in.
