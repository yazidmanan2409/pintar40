# Z · V2: words for a retake in progress (Daisy, 23 Nov 2026)

> **What holds now:**
> - **Yes, a line is needed.** Without it, a learner who has begun a retake sees only the earlier verdict. They may think the retake was lost, or that it doesn't count, and a teacher may read the earlier verdict as current.
> - **One line, neutral tone, beside the summary.** It appears only while a retake of that chapter's Uji Penguasaan is open. Once the retake has ended, it goes.
> - **For the owner's approval with Z's words.** DAISY-25 checked: no promise of notice.

| Where | ID | EN |
|---|---|---|
| Learner (the result summary and Hasil Belajar), new `hasil.ulang_berjalan` | «Anda sedang mengerjakan Uji Penguasaan ini lagi. Sampai selesai, yang tampil di sini hasil sebelumnya.» | «You are taking this Mastery Check again. Until you finish, this shows your previous result.» |
| Teacher (the per-learner page and the class view), new `mengajar.murid1.ulang_berjalan` | «Sedang mengerjakan Uji Penguasaan ini lagi. Sampai selesai, yang tampil hasil sebelumnya.» | «Taking this Mastery Check again. Until it is finished, the previous result is shown.» |

**Why this wording:**
- **«hasil sebelumnya» states only what is shown.** It doesn't say the retake will replace the result, or how. The V2 reader decides that, so the words make no promise about it.
