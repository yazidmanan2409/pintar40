# Dr Gladys's walkthrough · the observer's sheet (D-117)

For Yazid, or whoever sits with her. **Not for Dr Gladys.** She gets only her guide
(`docs/onboarding/PINTAR-Panduan-Walkthrough-Sekolah-Demo.md`).

**The question:** can Dr Gladys use and understand PINTAR as it exists, without the team explaining what we intended?

## Before she starts

- **Your own pass tonight** is inside the pause (D-117(a)). Whatever you change in the demo school is what she starts
  from: if you add a student, sit a test or re-issue a card, note it. Keep your own impressions apart from hers.
- Her account: added by you on staging as «Admin sekolah» of Sekolah Demo PINTAR. Note every role she holds; a role added
  later (for example «Dekan») is a condition of the evidence.
- Give her the guide and nothing else: no demonstration, no tour, no screenshot of where things are.
- If she agrees: record the screen and her voice while she thinks aloud. All the data is invented. A recording keeps her
  words exactly; notes are the fallback.
- Note the date, start time, device, browser, and whether you sit with her or she works alone.

## While she works

**Don't lead.** Don't point. Don't name a button, a menu or a screen. Don't explain what something means. Don't say whether
she is right. Don't explain what we intended. If she asks "why is it like this?", write the question down and answer it
after the session, if at all.

**Neutral prompts only**, and only when she goes quiet: «Apa yang sedang Ibu pikirkan?» · «Apa yang Ibu cari?»

**Help only when she genuinely cannot continue:** she asks for help, or says she is stuck. Before you help, write down:
`HELP REQUIRED`, the time, where she was, what she was trying to do, her words, and then the exact help you gave. Give the
smallest help that lets her continue.

**Watch for:**
- hesitation;
- going somewhere unexpected;
- not finding the next action;
- unclear words;
- "what does this mean?" and "what happens next?";
- missing an important action;
- misreading a status or a report;
- ordinary behaviour we did not plan for;
- an action that gets no acknowledgement or no next step;
- delight: "this works naturally".

Hesitation is an observation, not a defect.

**Write each observation as it happens, her words first:**

| Time | Where (screen, or the address bar) | Role | What she tried | What happened | Her words, as she said them | What she expected, only if she said it | Continued alone? / help given |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | |

Write her words in the language she used. Don't translate them into engineering words ("navigation issue") and don't
add an expectation she did not state.

**Don't fix, promise or argue.** Nothing changes during the walkthrough: no fix, no data change, no "we'll change that".

## If staging breaks

A genuine break is an error page, being unable to sign in at all, or data that has gone.
1. **Stop that journey.**
2. Screenshot it (with any «Kode rujukan»), note the time, and tell the coordinator.
3. The coordinator records it and reports to you. **Nothing is repaired silently and continued.**

She may carry on with journeys the break does not touch.
